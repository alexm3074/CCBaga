CCBaga is a free, open-source desktop toolkit that helps you keep your computer tidy and your working environment easy to check. It runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux, and it ships as two companion apps — **CCBaga-A** and **CCBaga-B** — so you can separate your everyday setup from your verification setup.

You do not need to know how to program to use it. If you can download a file and double-click it, you can use CCBaga.

---

## 👋 What Is CCBaga?

Most people end up with a computer that has quietly collected years of clutter: old profiles, mismatched browser settings, half-remembered system tweaks, and network configurations nobody remembers changing. CCBaga gives you one place to look at those things, tidy them up, and confirm that what you set is actually what is running.

Think of it as a **checklist and a cleaning crew in the same window**. You open it, you see the state of your machine in plain language, and you decide what to keep.

CCBaga is available in English and Simplified Chinese. The language can be switched at any time from the app's settings menu.

---

## ✨ What CCBaga Does for You

- **Keeps your workspace clean.** Old profiles and leftover settings are grouped together so you can review them and remove what you no longer need.
- **Lets you verify your environment.** Instead of trusting that a setting "should" be applied, CCBaga shows you the current state so you can confirm it.
- **Separates work from personal use.** Two independent apps mean one can stay untouched and stable while you experiment with the other.
- **Works the same everywhere.** The Windows, macOS, and Linux versions share the same layout, so switching computers does not mean relearning the app.
- **Stays out of your way.** No account is required, nothing runs in the background after you close it, and there is no subscription.

## 🅰️🅱️ The Two Apps: CCBaga-A and CCBaga-B

CCBaga is not one program but two, installed side by side. They share the same interface and the same tools, but they keep their settings in separate places.

| | **CCBaga-A** | **CCBaga-B** |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Your everyday, long-term setup | Testing changes before you commit |
| **Typical use** | The profile you actually work in | A clean copy you can reset freely |
| **If something breaks** | Restore from a backup | Delete and start over with no loss |

The reason for two apps is simple: if you only have one, every experiment risks your working setup. With two, you can try something in B, confirm it behaves the way you expect, and only then apply it in A.

You can install just one of them if you prefer. They do not interfere with each other.

## 🧭 A Closer Look at the Main Tools

**Claude identities.** CCBaga manages the set of saved identities you use with Claude-based tools. You can see which identity is currently active, switch between them, and remove ones you no longer need. Everything is stored locally on your own computer — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**Browser fingerprints.** Every browser presents a small set of characteristics to websites, such as your screen size and language. CCBaga shows you what your browser is currently reporting and lets you keep those values consistent between sessions, so your environment looks the same each time you open it.

**System settings.** A single page that gathers the operating-system settings CCBaga can read and adjust, with a plain-language explanation next to each one. You can see the current value, change it, and see confirmation that the change took effect.

**Network Skill.** A guided helper for checking and adjusting your network configuration. It reports what it finds in ordinary words — "connected", "not reachable", "slow response" — rather than error codes, and it suggests a next step for each finding.

CCBaga was built with people who live, study, or work away from their home country in mind. When you move between networks, countries, and devices, your digital environment drifts: settings change without you noticing, and it becomes hard to tell whether your machine is in the state you intended.

CCBaga exists to make that state visible and consistent. It is a personal environment-management tool.
